# Positions: Anu Bradford

Built from the limited corpus dated 2020–2026. The evidence focuses on EU law and digital governance. Business topics without direct evidence are explicitly marked as unrecorded; they must not be inferred from her legal scholarship.

## EU law and technology

- **Regulation and innovation:** not a necessary tradeoff; work on the ecosystem conditions for innovation while maintaining rights protections. Quote: "Se puede crecer, innovar y proteger los derechos de los ciudadanos al mismo tiempo." (S020, 2025-07-05).
- **GDPR and the AI Act:** do not assume repeal or non-enforcement will produce tech champions; diagnose the market and capital constraints. Quote: "Les Européens peuvent et doivent construire les autres piliers de l’écosystème technologique pour renforcer la compétitivité de l’Union" (S023, 2025-02-11).
- **EU content moderation:** the DSA can affect practices beyond the EU, though the extent depends on company implementation. Quote: "They also affect content moderation practices, even outside of the EU" (S009, date not provided).
- **Enforcement:** legal victory is hollow without implementation and observable market outcomes. Quote: "Victory is hollow if the EU cannot actually entrench regulations into concrete market outcomes." (S013, 2024-01-24).
- **Technology innovation constraints:** prioritize a unified digital market, integrated capital, lower costs of entrepreneurial failure, and global talent. (S020, 2025-07-05; S021, 2026-05-27; S022, 2025; S023, 2025-02-11).
- **AI development:** prioritize adoption and useful applications that fit European industrial strengths, rather than simply duplicating the US frontier-model approach. (S021, 2026-05-27).
- **Digital sovereignty:** total independence is not attainable; prioritize critical technologies, diversify, and build trusted partnerships. Quote: "And in that sense this idea of the complete technological sovereignty is an illusion." (S021, 2026-05-27).
- **Digital empires:** the US market-driven, Chinese state-driven, and EU rights-driven approaches remain analytically distinct, though contemporary politics can blur them. Quote: "We have the American market-driven model, the Chinese state-driven model, and the European rights-driven model." (S011, 2023-11-06).
- **EU market leverage:** use access to the single market as bargaining power; the market matters to large foreign firms. Quote: "For many of these tech giants, it's 20, 25% of their revenue." (S021, 2026-05-27).
- **Big tech incentives:** do not rely on firms to supply public-interest constraints voluntarily. Quote: "Y como su motivación es el beneficio, no responden al interés público." (S020, 2025-07-05).
- **Regulatory uncertainty:** inconsistent or unpredictable policy makes investment planning harder. Quote: "El mayor arancel de todos es la incertidumbre." (S020, 2025-07-05).
- **Cross-policy coordination:** coordinate technology, trade, defense, and economic security rather than treating them as isolated dossiers. (S021, 2026-05-27).
