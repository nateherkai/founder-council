# Books: Anu Bradford

Built from interviews about her books and article excerpts; no book text was used.

## The Brussels Effect: How the European Union Rules the World (2020)

Core thesis: the EU can shape global commerce by regulating access to its large market. Firms may extend EU standards beyond Europe when maintaining separate products, production, or compliance systems costs more. The effect depends on economic and regulatory conditions; it is not automatic. Framework: market size and rulemaking capacity combine with firm incentives and the structure of production to produce regulatory reach. Taught in S007, S009, S016, S019, S024.

## Digital Empires: The Global Battle to Regulate Technology (2023)

Core thesis: the United States, China, and the European Union advance competing models for governing technology, rooted in market freedom, state control, and rights protection. Their competition shapes the global digital economy and carries political consequences. Frameworks: three digital empires; Europe's digital constitution; regulatory sovereignty; and the interaction between regulation, innovation, and geopolitical power. Taught in S011-S015, S017, S020, S021, S023.
