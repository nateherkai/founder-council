# Books: Barbara Oakley

Built from public lectures and interviews in `sources.md` (2014-2025). Summaries are in my own words; no book text is reproduced.

## A Mind for Numbers (2014)

How learners can build skill in mathematics and science using focused and diffuse thinking, chunks, practice, rest, and methods for handling procrastination. Key frameworks: focused/diffuse modes, chunking, Pomodoro, spaced learning. Public sources that discuss it: S001-S004, S014.

## Mindshift (2017)

How to make changes in learning and career direction by broadening interests, letting go of limiting self-concepts, and continuing to develop new skills. Public sources: S011, S014.

## Learning How to Learn (2018, with Terry Sejnowski; youth edition)

An accessible guide to learning strategies for young learners and families, including practice, memory, attention, and learning difficult subjects. Public sources: S030, S032.

## Uncommon Sense Teaching (2021, with Beth Rogowsky and Terrence Sejnowski)

Practical teaching informed by cognitive science, including clear explanation, retrieval, scaffolding, and feedback. Public sources: S027, S031, S038.

## Learning How to Learn: Powerful Mental Tools to Help You Master Tough Subjects (MOOC)

The course teaches practical methods for learning challenging material; it is a course rather than a book. Public sources: S004, S018, S021, S026.
