# Positions: Barbara Oakley

Built from public interviews, lectures, research, and writing in `sources.md` (2013-2026). Dates are from source publication or recording metadata. Business topics without evidence are marked plainly.

## Learning and teaching

- **How people learn:** alternate focused effort with diffuse/resting thought. Quote: "when you're learning, you want to go back and forth between these modes" (S001, 2014).
- **Procrastination:** make the start finite with a Pomodoro interval, then reward focused effort. Quote: "Anybody can do 25 minutes, virtually anyone." (S001, 2014).
- **Breaks:** rest can support background processing. Quote: "But actually, your brain is doing a lot of productive, yet subconscious, work when you're taking these breaks." (S004, 2018).
- **Math ability:** learning difficult subjects is possible with appropriate methods and sustained practice. (S001, S004, S030-S031; 2014-2025).
- **Novice instruction:** use clear instruction, examples, and scaffolding before expecting novices to discover complex material independently. (S031, S036, S038; 2023-2025).
- **Practice:** prefer retrieval and spaced practice to passive rereading and cramming. (S002-S004, S030-S033; 2014-2025).
- **Transfer:** build fluent foundations, then vary practice and test use in new contexts. (S030-S033, S038; 2023-2025).
- **Learning technology:** choose tools to solve a specific instructional problem. (S010, S012-S014, S033; 2022-2025).
- **AI:** AI can help generate practice and explanations, but it should not replace the learner's thinking. (S010, S012-S014, S033-S034; 2024-2026).
- **Online learning:** carefully designed online courses can offer access at scale; design and engagement matter. (S018, S021, S026; 2016-2018).
- **Neuroscience for educators:** teachers should understand learning science and apply it carefully. (S027-S028; 2017-2020).
- **Passion and specialization:** follow interests while broadening them to avoid a narrow career box. Quote: "it's great to follow your passion, but broaden it also" (S004, 2018).
- **Group work:** give teams clear roles and accountability. (S017; 2004).
- **Cramming:** no recorded position stated as a broad policy; her learning guidance favors distributed practice over last-minute study (S002-S004).
