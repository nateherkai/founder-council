# books.md: Bruce Schneier

Built from the books page and references in the limited corpus. These are high-level thesis notes, not summaries of book text. The accessible source list does not contain the full books.

- **Applied Cryptography** (1994): a practical introduction to cryptographic algorithms and protocols. Listed on Schneier's books page (S012).
- **Secrets and Lies** (2000): security depends on systems, people, and operations, not cryptography alone. Referenced in S012 and S015.
- **Beyond Fear** (2003): evaluate security measures by their actual benefits, costs, and trade-offs. Listed on S012.
- **Schneier on Security** (2008): essays on computer security, privacy, and security trade-offs. Listed on S012.
- **Liars and Outliers** (2012): social trust is supported by morals, reputation, law, and security technologies. Discussed in S002.
- **Data and Goliath** (2015): surveillance and data collection create power imbalances and privacy risks. Listed on S012 and discussed in S013–S014.
- **Click Here to Kill Everybody** (2018): connected products and infrastructure require security to be treated as a whole-system public problem. Discussed in S015–S016.
- **Rebooting AI** (2019, with Gary Marcus): no recorded thesis in sufficient detail in this corpus.
- **A Hacker's Mind** (2023): no recorded thesis in sufficient detail in this corpus.
