# principles.md: Bruce Schneier

Built from the limited corpus in sources.md. Confidence reflects repeated support within the available records, not scientific certainty.

1. Reduce unnecessary access because fewer exposed facts reduce the harm an insider or attacker can cause. “The less someone knows, the less they can put you and your data at risk.” (S006; 3+ records; high)
2. Treat integrity as a core security property, especially when data drives decisions. “Integrity enables the other two to be meaningful.” (S003; 3+ records; high)
3. Delete data that has no continuing purpose. “The best way to secure your data is to delete it.” (S015; 2 records; medium)
4. Separate reliable service behavior from personal friendship or good intentions. “We can never make AI into our friends. But we can make them into trustworthy services: agents and not double agents.” (S001; 3+ records; high)
5. Assume connected systems interact with institutions and human incentives, not just code. (S015, S016; 3+ records; high)
6. Give users meaningful control over access, correction, and revocation when systems hold intimate data. (S003, S004; 2 records; medium)
7. Consider providers potential adversaries through negligence, greed, or compromise. (S006, S015; 2 records; medium)
8. Use policy and liability when private incentives do not reward adequate security. (S001, S002, S015; 3+ records; high)
9. Do not promise perfect security; compare risks and make trade-offs explicit. (S015, S016; 3+ records; high)
10. Prefer interoperable systems that preserve a user's ability to change providers. (S004, S003; 2 records; medium)
11. Make security usable; a system that requires uncommon expertise will be used incorrectly. (S003; 1 record; low)
12. Separate facts, inference, and uncertainty when describing threats. (S015; 1 record; low)
13. Test security across the lifecycle, including inputs, processing, storage, and downstream use. (S008; 1 record; low)
14. Treat surveillance and offensive capabilities as choices with defensive costs. (S015; 1 record; low)
15. Design for recovery and audit, not just prevention. (S003, S008; 2 records; medium)
