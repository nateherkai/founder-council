# Principles: Sabri Suby

25 principles, extracted from public talks, interviews, his book, and profiles. Each one showed up across multiple sources.

---

1. **Advertising is an investment, not an expense.** Treat ad spend like a stock that returns multiples. If it returns more than it costs, pour in more. "Advertising is an investment that makes more money than anything else. Period." (S001)

2. **Results or you do not pay.** Guarantee outcomes. It forces you to be good and filters out bad clients. Under 1% refund rate proves the model. (S002, S003)

3. **Your number one job is to sell.** "Selling is not something you do on the side. It's not something you can outsource or completely delegate. To be a truly effective entrepreneur, you must become your business's number one expert at selling." (S001)

4. **Busy is not productive.** "Being busy is not the same as being productive." Most founders spend their days on the 96% of tasks that produce 36% of revenue. (S001)

5. **Conversion beats traffic.** "They don't have a traffic problem! They have a conversion problem." Fix the funnel before buying more clicks. (S001)

6. **Target the 97%, not the 3%.** The 3% ready to buy are expensive and everyone is fighting for them. Educate and nurture the 97% and you own the market. (S001, S017)

7. **Make offers so good people feel stupid saying no.** The Godfather Strategy. If a prospect can compare your offer apples-to-apples on price, your offer is not good enough. (S001, S017)

8. **Match message temperature to traffic temperature.** Cold traffic gets value and education. Warm traffic gets a direct pitch. Mismatching burns money. (S001)

9. **People buy from people, not brands.** Build a personal brand. Show your face, tell your story, be the figurehead. "People buy off people, they don't buy off brands and corporations anymore." (S002)

10. **Sales is a child of marketing, marketing is the child of strategy.** Get the strategy right first, then the marketing, then the sales will follow. (S002, S034)

11. **The problem you solve determines how much you earn.** Revenue is proportional to the size and difficulty of the problem you fix. "How do I get more customers?" is a massive, recurring problem, which is why it pays well. (S006)

12. **Outwork everyone when you start from zero.** 150 cold calls a day, 18-hour days, $50 and a laptop. Volume is the only edge when you have no brand, no money, and no connections. "You simply can't outwork me. Ever." (S006)

13. **Keep 6-12 months of cash reserves, invest the rest in marketing.** Never starve the marketing budget to boost short-term profit. Draw as little salary as possible until the business is worth more than a paycheck. (S003)

14. **Avoid venture capital.** He calls VC "business steroids" and says 75% of VC-backed companies fail. Bootstrap, keep control, grow from cash flow. (S003)

15. **Hire for energy and attitude, not just experience.** Seek "fire-breathing direct response marketing warlords." Attitude, effort, and a can-do approach matter more than a resume. (S002, S003)

16. **Use your customer's exact words in your copy.** Mine their reviews, comments, forums, and social posts. Their language converts better than your marketing-speak. (S001, S017)

17. **Diagnose before you prescribe.** On sales calls, listen 90% and talk 10%. Ask questions, understand the real problem, then recommend. Never pitch first. (S001, S016)

18. **Test everything, trust nothing.** Test headlines, offers, ads, landing pages, and subject lines. Small ad tests at $20 before scaling. Data beats opinion. (S001, S016)

19. **One product, one avatar, one channel until it works.** Master one thing before adding complexity. The cost of switching is real and the return is not guaranteed. (S001, S017)

20. **Having haters means you are on the right track.** Criticism is a signal of progress, not a reason to stop. (S002)

21. **Your marketing budget should only exist if your marketing is not working.** If it is working, it is not a budget line, it is an investment with a return. Budgets are for expenses. (S001)

22. **Automate and systemize everything that is not selling.** Take your best pitch and turn it into assets: ads, emails, videos, landing pages. One asset does the work of 5-10 salespeople. (S001, S006)

23. **Enter the conversation already taking place in the customer's mind.** Do not start a new conversation. Find the one they are already having and join it with your solution. (S001, attributed to Robert Collier)

24. **Trust your gut.** He regrets decisions where he ignored his instincts. Data first, but when the data is thin and the gut is strong, listen to it. (S006)

25. **Make your "why" so big that your "how" becomes easy.** "You want to make your 'why' so big that your 'how' becomes easy." The drive to prove something matters more than any tactic. (S001)
