# principles.md: Atul Gawande

Built from 39 public articles and interviews from 1998 to 2026. Confidence reflects distinct supporting sources in this corpus.

1. **Define the outcome before choosing the intervention.** “We don’t ask what our town’s most important health needs are, let alone make a concerted effort to tackle them.” (S001). 4 sources. High. (S001, S015, S020, S029)
2. **Prevent disease as well as treating it.** “We would have made a priority of preventing disease, rather than just treating it.” (S001). 3 sources. High. (S001, S003, S009)
3. **Judge spending by outcomes and distribution, not amount alone.** (S001, S015). 2 sources. Medium.
4. **Use data to find variation, then learn why it exists.** (S015, S020, S021). 3 sources. High.
5. **Do not equate more care with better care.** (S008, S015, S035, S038). 4 sources. High.
6. **Protect patient choice in matters that depend on personal priorities.** (S013, S022, S027). 3 sources. High.
7. **Use short checklists for predictable omissions in complex work.** (S017, S024, S026). 3 sources. High.
8. **Make it safe for every team member to raise a concern.** (S017, S024, S027). 3 sources. High.
9. **Do not expect evidence alone to change professional habits.** People need trusted peers, practical coaching, and time to adapt. (S010, S025, S027). 3 sources. High.
10. **A new practice can harm patients before the team masters it.** “No matter how accomplished, surgeons trying something new got worse before they got better” (S021). 2 sources. Medium. (S021, S032)
11. **Track outcomes while learning.** (S021, S024). 2 sources. Medium.
12. **Treat mistakes as a signal to improve the process and the practitioner.** “we can make dramatic improvements by going after the process, not the people.” (S023). 3 sources. High. (S017, S023, S038)
13. **Do not confuse busyness with quality.** Volume and reputation are weak proxies for results. (S008, S015, S038). 3 sources. High.
14. **Be honest about uncertainty and limits.** (S013, S021, S022). 3 sources. High.
15. **Measure the work that matters to patients, including burdens and harms.** (S008, S013, S020). 3 sources. High.
16. **Build the local workforce and relationships needed to deliver change.** (S001, S010, S012). 3 sources. High.
17. **Do not let standardization erase individual goals.** Inferred from S013, S022, S024.
18. **Safety is not the same as a meaningful life.** Balance protection with the person's goals. (S013, S018, S029). 3 sources. High.
19. **Technology must fit clinical work and demonstrably help patients.** (S004, S024). 2 sources. Medium.
20. **When a clinician is unsafe, protect patients and arrange clear review and support.** (S038). 1 source. Low.
