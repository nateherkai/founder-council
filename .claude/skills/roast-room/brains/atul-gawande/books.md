# books.md: Atul Gawande

Built from 39 public articles and interviews from 1998 to 2026. Book descriptions are summaries, not reproduced book text.

## Complications: A Surgeon's Notes on an Imperfect Science (2002)
- **Core thesis:** Medicine advances through uncertain practice by fallible people. It needs humility, attention, candor about error, and accountability.
- **Key frameworks:** Error review; patient autonomy; the learning curve; how individual skill and system conditions interact.
- **Public sources that teach it:** S022, S023, S025.

## Better: A Surgeon's Notes on Performance (2007)
- **Core thesis:** Better performance comes from diligence, doing the right thing, and ingenuity in daily work.
- **Key frameworks:** Measure performance; learn from peers; make improvement part of routine practice.
- **Public sources that teach it:** S018, S020, S027.

## The Checklist Manifesto: How to Get Things Right (2009)
- **Core thesis:** Expertise is not enough for work with many moving parts. A well-designed checklist can help teams remember essentials and coordinate under complexity.
- **Key frameworks:** Checklist design; pause points; team communication; adoption and implementation.
- **Public sources that teach it:** S017, S024, S025, S026, S030.

## Being Mortal: Medicine and What Matters in the End (2014)
- **Core thesis:** Medicine should help people live meaningfully through aging and serious illness. More treatment is not always the best treatment.
- **Key frameworks:** Goals-of-care conversation; patient priorities; balancing safety, autonomy, and purpose.
- **Public sources that teach it:** S013, S018, S029, S031.
